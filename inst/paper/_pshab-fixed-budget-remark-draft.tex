The following observation upgrades an assumed approximation-decay condition to
a consequence of a named smoothness class, while remaining purely
approximation-theoretic.

\begin{remark}[Fixed-budget PSHAB approximation]\label{rem:pshab-fixed-budget}
Let $\cT_{\bar L}^{\circ}$ denote the class of axis-aligned, leaf-constant tree
functions on $[0,1]^d$ with at most $\bar L$ leaves -- the continuum analogue
of the finite-grid class $\cT_{\bar L}$ used above. Define the population
oracle errors
\[
  \delta_e^*(\bar L)
  :=\inf_{f\in\cT_{\bar L}^{\circ}}\Ltwo{f-\ez},
  \qquad
  \delta_\mu^*(\bar L)
  :=\inf_{f\in\cT_{\bar L}^{\circ}}\Lw{f-\mz}.
\]

For each $j\in\{e,\mu\}$, suppose $\gamma_{0,j}$ (where $\gamma_{0,e}=\ez$ and
$\gamma_{0,\mu}=\mz$) belongs to the piecewise sparse anisotropic Besov
class of \citet[Definition~4.5]{xu2026}, with sparsity $s_j$, anisotropic
smoothness indices at most one, and harmonic-mean active-coordinate smoothness
$\bar\alpha_j$. \citet[Theorems~5.5 and~6.1]{xu2026} give an explicit
anisotropic dyadic-grid tree construction achieving, for squared-error loss
and \emph{every} integer $L\ge1$ (their achievability bound is uniform in $L$;
the constant depends on the smoothness-class parameters but not on $L$ or
$n$), squared population approximation error of order $L^{-2\bar\alpha_j/s_j}$
over the active coordinates. Evaluating this achievability bound at the fixed
budget $\bar L$ gives, for every integer $\bar L\ge1$,
\[
  \delta_j^*(\bar L)
  =O\!\left(\bar L^{-\bar\alpha_j/s_j}\right),
  \qquad j\in\{e,\mu\},
\]
and consequently
\[
  \delta_e^*(\bar L)\,\delta_\mu^*(\bar L)
  \;\lesssim\;
  \bar L^{-\bar\alpha_e/s_e-\bar\alpha_\mu/s_\mu}.
\]
Here the constants are independent of $\bar L$ and $n$.  In particular, these
inequalities may be evaluated at the paper's \emph{fixed} budget $\bar L$;
the order notation records the deterministic envelope across possible budgets
and does not introduce a sequence $\bar L_n\to\infty$.

\textbf{Oracle scope.}
This statement bounds only the best possible population approximation error:
it describes what is attainable \emph{if one could choose the best
$\bar L$-leaf tree}.  It makes no claim that the finite-sample penalized-ERM
tree $\that_j$ attains, or nearly attains, this bound.  Relating this population
ceiling to the realized bias of the fitted nuisance is a separate
estimation-and-inference problem not addressed here.

\begin{proof}
\emph{Proof strategy.}
Apply the squared-error achievability bound of \citet[Thms.~5.5,
6.1]{xu2026} separately to the two nuisances, evaluate it at $L=\bar L$, pass
from squared error to norm error by taking square roots, and multiply the
resulting bounds.

By the cited achievability result, for each $j\in\{e,\mu\}$ there is a
constant $C_j<\infty$, depending on the PSHAB-class parameters but
\emph{not on $L$ or $n$}, such that
\[
  \inf_{f\in\cT_L^\circ}
  \Ltwo{f-\gamma_{0,j}}^2
  \le C_jL^{-2\bar\alpha_j/s_j}.
\]
This is precisely the clause permitting evaluation at a fixed leaf budget.
Setting $L=\bar L$ gives
\[
  \bigl\{\delta_e^*(\bar L)\bigr\}^2
  \le C_e\bar L^{-2\bar\alpha_e/s_e},
  \qquad
  \delta_e^*(\bar L)
  \le C_e^{1/2}\bar L^{-\bar\alpha_e/s_e}.
\]
For the outcome nuisance, $0\le1-\ez(X)\le1$ implies, for every
$f\in\cT_{\bar L}^{\circ}$,
\[
  \Lw{f-\mz}^2
  =\E\!\left[\{1-\ez(X)\}\{f(X)-\mz(X)\}^2\right]
  \le\E\!\left[\{f(X)-\mz(X)\}^2\right]
  =\Ltwo{f-\mz}^2.
\]
Taking infima and applying the achievability bound to the right-hand side
therefore yields
\[
  \bigl\{\delta_\mu^*(\bar L)\bigr\}^2
  \le C_\mu\bar L^{-2\bar\alpha_\mu/s_\mu},
  \qquad
  \delta_\mu^*(\bar L)
  \le C_\mu^{1/2}\bar L^{-\bar\alpha_\mu/s_\mu}.
\]
Multiplication gives
\[
  \delta_e^*(\bar L)\delta_\mu^*(\bar L)
  \le (C_eC_\mu)^{1/2}
     \bar L^{-\bar\alpha_e/s_e-\bar\alpha_\mu/s_\mu},
\]
which is the claimed product bound.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Proof audit.}
\emph{Assumptions:} the PSHAB hypotheses are stated separately for both
nuisances.  \emph{Dependency:} only the cited external achievability result
and the elementary inequality $\Lw{\cdot}\le\Ltwo{\cdot}$ are used.
\emph{Rates:} taking square roots halves each squared-error exponent, and
multiplication adds the resulting exponents.
\emph{Edge cases:} weak overlap and outcome tails do not affect this
population upper bound; failure occurs if either nuisance is outside the stated
PSHAB class or if the admissible continuum tree class does not contain the
approximants certified by the cited construction.
\emph{Tightness:} the approximation order is inherited from that external
achievability result; no matching lower bound is asserted here.
\emph{Failure localization:} the unproved bridge is deliberately excluded:
nothing above transfers the oracle bound to the fitted tree $\that_j$.
\end{remark}

## Lens 1: Assumption Stress Test — Issues Found: 3

### Issue 1.1: Clipping scope unstated, contradicting `theory.tex`, and $\thetahat$ defined before the assumption that makes it finite
- **Location:** lines 199–211 (`eq:refit`, $\thetahat$ display) vs. lines 228–231 (`ass:construct`)
- **Severity:** CRITICAL (blocking)
- **Claim in text:** line 229–231 — *"\begin{assumption}[Clipping]\label{ass:construct} Fitted
  propensities are clipped so that $1-\hat e(\bx)\ge c$ for every $\bx$..."*
- **Problem:** Three defects converge:
  1. **Ordering.** Line 209 used $\hat w_{\hat\tau_e}=\hat e_{\hat\tau_e}/(1-\hat e_{\hat\tau_e})$
     inside the definition of $\thetahat$. With $\hat e_\tau$ as defined at line 199 — the raw
     within-leaf treated fraction — $\hat e_\tau(\bx)=1$ on any all-treated leaf, so $\hat w$ is
     $+\infty$ and $\thetahat$ is undefined. The assumption that rules this out arrived 20 lines
     *after* the object it rescues.
  2. **Scope contradicts `theory.tex`.** `theory.tex:234–235` restricts clipping to the reported
     $\ehat$; the manuscript's blanket phrasing admitted no such restriction.
  3. **That reading falsified two adjacent statements**: line 203's "empirical proportion
     treated" and line 217's "these projections are exactly the squared-error minimizers" — a
     clipped refit is the *constrained* minimiser, not the unconstrained one.
- **Suggested fix (as issued):** Move the clipping statement before the $\thetahat$ display and
  scope it: structure selection uses the unclipped leaf refits; clipping applies only to the
  $\hat e_{\hat\tau_e}$ that enters $\psi$.

### Issue 1.2: $m_n$ enters with no condition; $\cT^{(j)}_n$'s randomness and non-emptiness unaddressed; `theory.tex`'s no-controls fallback dropped
- **Location:** lines 180–184 (`eq:feasible`)
- **Severity:** MAJOR
- **Problem:** $m_n$ appeared as a bare undefined symbol. `theory.tex` records $\bar Lm_n\lesssim
  n$ as part of `ass:construct`(d) with a rationale (the constraint reconciles selection with the
  minimum-leaf-mass requirement) and a null-event fallback (parent-leaf control mean); none of
  this was in §2.4. $\cT^{(j)}_n$ is also a random set, and nothing guaranteed it nonempty.
  **Additional note:** `theory.tex:3167`'s own claim that "$m_n/n\to0$ because $\bar
  Lm_n\lesssim n$ with $\bar L$ fixed" does not actually follow ($\bar Lm_n\lesssim n$ with fixed
  $\bar L$ gives only $m_n/n\lesssim1/\bar L$, a constant, not $m_n=o(n)$) — flagged as an
  inherited defect, not introduced by §2.4.
- **Suggested fix:** Name $m_n$ as a minimum-leaf-count sequence with $1\le m_n\to\infty$ and
  $m_n=o(n)$ directly in §2.4; state $\cT^{(j)}_n$ is data-dependent and always contains the root
  partition; add the parent-leaf fallback.

### Issue 1.3: `ass:construct` is now a one-part assumption but is cited downstream as if it were `theory.tex`'s four-part construction
- **Location:** line 229 vs. lines 274, 410, 518
- **Severity:** MINOR
- **Problem:** `theory.tex:344–362`'s `ass:construct` has four parts (Loss, Leaf refit, Clipping,
  Leaf mass); the manuscript keeps only (c) under that label while `lem:selection`, `cor:variance`,
  `lem:biasbound` all cite "Assumption~\ref{ass:construct}." Coverage is mostly preserved by other
  means (loss/refit are stated elsewhere in the prose), but a reader tracing the citation alone
  cannot recover the full construction. **Not fixed in the same-session follow-up** — recorded as
  open in `notation.md`'s `ass:construct` entry.

## Lens 2: Derivation Verification — Issues Found: 3

### Issue 2.1: $R^{(\mu)}$ on the control-conditional scale `theory.tex` rules out, breaking the match to §2.1's $\|\cdot\|_{2,w}$
- **Location:** line 195 (`eq:risk-emp`), line 215 (`eq:risk-pop`)
- **Severity:** CRITICAL (blocking)
- **Problem:** `theory.tex:231–234` states the opposite convention without qualification: risks
  are on the unnormalised scale, so $R^{(\mu)}$ matches $\|\cdot\|_{2,w}$, *not* its
  control-conditional average. `theory.tex:3116–3121` explains why: the conditional-average scale
  puts $R^{(\mu)}_n$'s limit off by a factor of $(1-\pi)^{-1}$ relative to $\widehat q_\ell$,
  silently making `prop:selection-rate`'s constant $c_1$ acquire a $\pi$-dependence the statement
  denies, and breaking the margin-to-norm identity `lem:biasbound`'s $D_\mu=\|\hat\mu-\mu_0\|_{2,w}$
  relies on. Noted in fairness: *within* the manuscript, `eq:risk-emp` and `eq:risk-pop` were
  mutually consistent — the defect was the mismatch with `theory.tex` and §2.1's own norm, not
  internal incoherence.
- **Suggested fix:** Restate on the unnormalised, control-weighted scale:
  $R^{(\mu)}_n(\tau)=\Prob_n[(1-A)\{Y-\hat\mu_\tau(\bX)\}^2]$.

### Issue 2.2: "$\psi$ is affine and strictly monotone in $\theta$" is false as stated
- **Location:** line 211
- **Severity:** MINOR
- **Problem:** From `eq:score`, $\partial\psi/\partial\theta=-A/\pi$, which is zero for every
  control observation, so $\psi$ is affine but not strictly monotone pointwise. The property that
  actually delivers uniqueness is that $\theta\mapsto\Prob_n\psi$ is affine with slope
  $-\hat\pi/\pi$, nonzero exactly when $\hat\pi>0$ — which also explains why the conclusion
  depends on $\hat\pi>0$ in the first place, a dependence the original stated reason could not
  explain. The same phrasing recurs at line 477 for $\E[\psi]$ (slope $-1$, conclusion fine there,
  pointwise claim equally false) — out of scope for a §2.4-only pass, flagged for later.

### Issue 2.3: Propensity selection criterion and the bias-governing norm are in different norms, no bridge stated
- **Location:** lines 192–196, 212–217
- **Severity:** MINOR (non-blocking)
- **Problem:** $R^{(e)}$ used unweighted squared error in $e$ (margin
  $\|e_0-\Pi_\tau e_0\|_2^2$), but `lem:biasbound` controls bias via
  $D_w=\|\hat w-w_0\|_{2,w}$ — control-weighted, in the odds $w$. §2.4 never noted the gap. It is
  closable (the two norms are equivalent since $c\le1-e_0\le1$, and $e\mapsto e/(1-e)$ is
  Lipschitz on the clipped range — `theory.tex:517–520`, `:2788` carry the conversion), but was
  unremarked.

## Positive Findings

1. **`eq:refit` ↔ `eq:risk-emp` ↔ `eq:risk-pop` cohere exactly, and the $\Pi^\nu_\tau$ choice is
   precisely right.** Verified by direct computation for both the propensity minimiser
   ($\Pi_\tau e_0$) and the outcome minimiser (identically $\Pi^\nu_\tau\mu_0$ under
   $d\nu=\{1-e_0\}d\Prob$) — the control-weighted projection is not a stylistic choice, it is the
   *only* projection making the population risk the correct limit of the empirical one.
2. **The `eq:feasible` asymmetry is correct and easy to get wrong.** $\cT^{(\mu)}_n$ constrains
   $|\ell|_0$ while $\cT^{(e)}_n$ constrains $|\ell|$ — exactly matching which observations each
   leaf plug-in in `eq:refit` consumes. Faithful to `theory.tex:221–222`.
3. **The $\thetahat$ display is algebraically exact.** Solving the estimating equation with
   `eq:score` reproduces the displayed formula precisely; the $1/\pi$ cancels and $\hat\pi$
   emerges naturally. The uniqueness *conclusion* was correct even though its stated *reason* was
   not (Issue 2.2).
4. **`rem:two-trees-why`'s central technical claim is genuinely supported, not assertion-only.**
   `prop:bilinear` is stated for any $\eta=(e,\mu)$ satisfying the positivity bound, no tree
   structure anywhere in its hypothesis, and `lem:biasbound` bounds the bias in exactly the
   $D_w,D_\mu$ notation the remark cites.
5. **The remark is admirably careful where it matters most.** The hedges ("not by itself a
   validity requirement," "necessary but not sufficient on its own," "not in general covered by
   this argument") are exactly what keeps the argument honest, and the Hahn (1998) attribution is
   accurate. The problem was that §3.5 later dropped them (line 684), not that §2.4 lacked them.

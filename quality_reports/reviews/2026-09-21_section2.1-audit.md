## Lead that went in, and what it turned out to be

The audit was commissioned with a specific lead: that `cor:variance`'s proof invokes "the
uniformly bounded conditional second moment of $Y$" without that condition ever appearing in its
stated hypothesis chain. **This lead was refuted.** The condition is stated verbatim in
`lem:selection`'s own hypothesis list and is correctly inherited through
`cor:variance → prop:P-instantiation1 → lem:uniform → lem:selection`. The apparent gap traced to
`notation.md`'s own stale line references (pointing at `:242` instead of the condition's actual
location), not to anything wrong in the manuscript. No manuscript change was needed for this lead
itself; `notation.md`'s stale-line-number problem is a separate, real finding (see below).

## What was fixed, and why

**MAJOR — `cor:variance`'s proof had an unjustified same-data LLN interchange.** The original
proof asserted $\hat V(\tau_e,\tau_\mu)\to_p V$ via "the law of large numbers... then give," but
the summands depend on $n$ through the fixed-partition leaf refits — a plug-in-inside-an-LLN step,
not a plain LLN, and the same pattern a `proof-auditor` pass had already caught and required
fixing elsewhere in this manuscript (`prop:pseudo-consistency`, per `claims.md`). Fixed by adding
the mechanism: `Assumption~\ref{ass:construct}` bounds $\hat w$ by $(1-c)/c$, and each summand is a
function of finitely many such bounded leaf-wise plug-ins, so the LLN applies leaf by leaf. In the
same edit, introduced the previously-undefined notation $\hat V(\tau_e,\tau_\mu)$ properly (the
proof had used it without ever having stated what the two arguments mean), paralleling how
`lem:uniform` already introduces $\hat\theta(\tau_e,\tau_\mu)$.

**Traceability fixes.** `cor:width` cited its nuisance conditions by description ("the moment,
construction, and fixed-budget conditions above") rather than by `\ref` — the one place in the
paper's hypothesis chain that departs from its own established pattern of citing predecessors by
label. Replaced with "the conditions of Proposition~\ref{lem:selection}".

**Dead/missing symbols.** $\cY$ (introduced at `:53` as "$Y\in\cY\subset\R$") was never used again
anywhere in the manuscript — confirmed by grep, not just by `notation.md`'s prior (also stale)
note. Dropped; §2.1 now writes "$Y\in\R$" directly. $m_1(\bx)$ was *worse* than `notation.md`
previously claimed: not "used only in prose" but used nowhere at all after its own definition.
Fixed by actually using it at its natural site, `thm:regular`'s proof-sketch ("never $m_1(\bx)$,
which would be undefined wherever $e_0(\bx)=0$"), which previously wrote the expectation longhand.
Conversely, $\to_p$ (convergence in probability) is used six times downstream — more than
$\rightsquigarrow$'s five — yet was never defined, despite $\rightsquigarrow$ being defined in the
very same sentence. Added three words to that sentence.

**Cross-reference completeness.** The *identified* (observed-data) form of the ATT,
$\theta_0=\E\{Y-\E(Y\mid\bX,A=0)\mid A=1\}$, had no label, so nothing could `\eqref` it — it was
restated longhand at `thm:regular` instead. Added `\label{eq:att-id}`; `thm:regular` now cites it
by reference rather than repeating the display.

**Bold/unbold notation drift.** `domain-reviewer` found the manuscript's dominant convention is
bold for the observation and covariate vector ($\bO$, $\bX$), but three spots used the unbold
capital form for the same objects: §2.1's own observation list ("$O_1,\ldots,O_n$", contradicting
$\bO_i$ twelve lines later in the same subsection), `eq:score`'s display ($\psi(O;\ldots)$,
$\mu(X)$, $w(X)$, contradicting every downstream invocation which uses $\psi(\bO;\ldots)$), and
`ass:causal`'s ignorability clause ("$\{Y(0),Y(1)\}\perp A\mid X$", contradicting the identified-ATT
display nine lines below it in the same subsection, which already uses $\bX$). All three fixed to
match the dominant bold convention. **Explicitly not touched:** a separate, more pervasive
lowercase $x$-vs-$\bx$ inconsistency that recurs throughout the document (e.g. `ass:causal`'s own
positivity display, "$1-e_0(x)\ge c$ for every $x\in\cX$", uses unbold $x$ while §2.1's definition
of $e_0$ two sentences earlier uses $e_0(\bx)$) — this was outside what was audited and reported to
the author, and fixing it now would be scope creep beyond the specific findings presented and
approved. Flagged here for a possible future pass.

**The "$w$" collision.** `notation.md` already documented that the propensity odds $w_0,w$ and the
control-weighted norm's $_{2,w}$ subscript share a letter, and judged this an acceptable, non-fixed
collision in general. The audit found one concrete site where it could mislead:
$D_w=\|\hat w-w_0\|_{2,w}$ (`lem:biasbound`) triple-loads the letter in about fifteen characters,
and a reader who reads the subscript as "weighted by $w_0$" gets the *wrong* weight — inverted
relative to the norm's actual $1-e_0$ weighting, which matters because that norm choice is the
lemma's entire point. Fixed at the cheaper of the two sites the reviewer proposed: a clarifying
half-sentence in §2.1 itself, where the norm is first defined, rather than a parenthetical
repeated at every use site.

**Proofreading (grammar/style, bundled into the same paragraph rewrites):** missing commas (after
"$\bO_n$", after "$e(\bx)$"); a comma-splice fixed to "and" before "$\gamma_{0,\mu}=\mu_0$";
trailing whitespace removed; "draw from the random variable" → "realization of the random
variable" (the former conventionally takes a distribution as its object, not a random variable);
consolidated a mid-sentence verb switch ("Define also $\pi=\ldots$, and **let** $m_1=\ldots$")
into one verb, and lightly reduced the section's drift across roughly eight near-synonymous
symbol-introduction verbs.

## Confirmed as NOT gaps (checked, no action needed)

- **ATT via potential outcomes (`eq:att`) vs. its identified form (`eq:att-id`):** ordinary,
  correct two-step identification exposition — the bridging sentence at `:133` does real work,
  gated on the explicitly named `ass:causal`. Collapsing the two would itself be the defect.
- **$Y\in\R$ with no boundedness claim:** correct as stated. Boundedness is a graded, locally
  scoped condition (weak moment condition for `lem:selection`'s limit result; strictly stronger
  boundedness only for `prop:selection-rate`'s exponential *rate*; neither imposed on
  `thm:anchor`, which needs neither) — constraining $\cY$ globally would make
  `prop:selection-rate`'s extra hypothesis vacuous and over-assume for `thm:anchor`.
- **The iid-in-prose vs. iid-as-a-checked-hypothesis pattern:** intentional and coherent — §2.1
  describes the sampling scheme; `lem:selection`'s hypothesis list re-declares it as a condition
  to check, alongside the moment condition. Every asymptotic result inherits it through that
  chain; nothing relies on the prose statement alone.

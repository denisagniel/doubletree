**Two corrections to the build's own working assumptions, caught by reading source
rather than trusting the plan:**
1. `estimate_att()` DOES reject non-binary `X` — the binary-coding gap
   `assert_binary_design_matrix()` actually closes is specifically in
   `estimate_att_crossfit()`, whose only `X` check is the shared `check_att_data()`,
   which validates `NA`s but not binariness.
2. Both estimators return a named `list`, not a tibble. Quartile discretization needs
   a bin-OCCUPANCY check, not just strictly-increasing cutpoints — a zero-inflated
   `prior_cost` can produce strictly-increasing cutpoints with an empty middle bin
   (`c(1,1,2,3)` → cuts `(1, 1.5, 2.25)`, both `1`s in Q1, Q2 empty).

## 16:20 — theory: one-sided sufficiency CLT

Resolved the open question `theory.tex` itself flags after `lem:cross`'s proof: when only
one nuisance is exactly sufficient (the other satisfies a one-sided restriction of
`ass:pseudo` — unique population-risk minimizer, no exact fit), is
$\sqrt n(\thetahat-\theta_0)$ still asymptotically normal? Answer: yes, in both directions,
via the same exact finite-sample leaf-refit identity `lem:cross`'s own proof already uses
(control-arm residuals sum to zero on any leaf where the sufficient side is exactly
leaf-constant) — not a rate/approximation argument. Added `Lemma lem:onesided` to
`theory.tex` (new `sec:onesided`, ~360 new lines): two cases (outcome-sufficient /
propensity-pseudo-true, and the mirror), two corollaries with closed-form variance and

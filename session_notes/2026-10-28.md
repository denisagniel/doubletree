**Goal.** Work out why doubletree's flagship tree-based ATT estimator did not
beat OLS on the first real-data application, build a disciplined framework
for when to recommend the method going forward, and design a small, honest

**What happened.** Oracle consultation (6 rounds, one continued session,
`ses_e7c484b49ffe7vLoPjQW2PyU9C`) diagnosed the real-data null:
`92_outcome_screen.R`'s own finding (xgboost beats additive OLS by ~0.2% of
outcome MSE on the 15 curated binary covariates) shows there was no
nonparametric signal available at any leaf budget -- not a `leaf_budget=16`
artifact. Reframed the paper's value proposition from "beats OLS" to

estimator switch (or outcome/application selection) is the same forbidden

one level up -- the anchor interval already supplies that insurance. Cataloged
all 16 `smidata` HEDIS/PQI quality measures; found and flagged separately

~240 cells) on the grounds that a wider search makes a credible illustration
